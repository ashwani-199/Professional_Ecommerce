Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

30-Day Returns

From the date of delivery

Hassle-Free

Simple return process

Fast Refunds

5-7 business days

1

Our Guarantee

  • Your satisfaction is our top priority — if you're unhappy, we'll make it right
  • 30-day return policy gives you plenty of time to decide
2

Return Eligibility

  • Returned within 30 days of the delivery date
  • Unused and in the same condition as received
  • In original packaging with all tags attached
  • Proof of purchase required (order number or receipt)
3

Non-Returnable Items

  • Gift cards
  • Opened personal care items (skincare, makeup)
  • Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
  • Worn, washed, or customer-damaged products
4

How to Start a Return

  • 1Contact us at support@americanowl.com or via contact form
  • 2Provide your order number and reason for return
  • 3Receive return authorization and prepaid shipping label
  • 4Ship the item back within 7 days using the provided label
5

Refund Timeline

Once we receive your return:

  • 1Inspection — We inspect the item within 2-3 business days
  • 2Approval — You'll receive an email confirmation once approved
  • 3Processing — Refund processed within 5-7 business days after approval
  • 4Posting — 3-5 additional business days to appear in your account
6

Refund Method

  • Refunds issued to the original payment method
  • Contact us if your original payment method is no longer available
7

Exchanges

  • Exchanges available for same item, different size or color
  • Subject to availability — contact our support team to request
  • Processed once the returned item is received and inspected
8

Damaged or Incorrect Items

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery for issues
  • We'll arrange a replacement or full refund including return shipping
  • Include photos of the damage for faster processing
9

Return Shipping Costs

  • Defective/Wrong item — We cover all return shipping costs
  • Change of mind — Small return shipping fee may apply
  • Prepaid return label provided for your convenience
10

Tips for a Smooth Return

  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging
  • Include all accessories, manuals, and tags
  • Keep your tracking number until your refund is processed
  • Contact us if your refund hasn't arrived within the expected timeframe
11

Questions?

  • Contact our support team — we're here to help
